# Research Watch: Meta Muse — Personal AI Agent

- Repo/Link: https://ai.meta.com/muse/
- Source: Hacker News (219 pts, #1)

## Why this is worth watching
Meta has launched Muse, a personal AI agent integrated across Meta's family of apps (WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, Ray-Ban smart glasses). At 219 HN points and the #1 slot, this represents the largest consumer-facing personal agent launch since ChatGPT. Meta's distribution advantage (3B+ monthly users) could make Muse the dominant personal agent for non-developer users.

## What stands out immediately
- Runs across WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, Ray-Ban glasses
- Memory across sessions ("remembers you")
- Multimodal: voice, vision, text
- Cloud-only, tied to Meta account — no offline/local mode
- Not developer-facing; no API announced yet

## Why clawfit should care
Muse is a consumer-oriented agent that is out of scope for clawfit's current developer/enterprise recommendation lens. However, its rise signals that the agent landscape is splitting into developer-first and consumer-first tracks. clawfit may need to explicitly label tools as "developer-facing" vs "consumer-facing" to avoid recommending Muse-tier tools to engineering personas.
