# Research Watch: phuryn/pm-skills — PM Skill Marketplace

- Repo/Link: https://github.com/phuryn/pm-skills
- Source: GitHub Trending (2026-06-10, 13,408★)

## Why this is worth watching
The pm-skills marketplace encodes 68 PM frameworks across 9 plugins (Discovery, Strategy, Execution, Market Research, Data Analytics, Go-to-Market, Marketing & Growth, Toolkit, AI Shipping), making Teresa Torres / Marty Cagan / Alberto Savoia methodology executable via slash commands. At 13k★ in trending, it is the first high-signal PM-specialist domain skill pack — distinct from the broader knowledge-work packs (Anthropic Knowledge Work Plugins, marketingskills) and from engineering-focused packs (addyosmani/agent-skills).

## What stands out immediately
- 68 skills across 9 PM-specific plugins, not a general-purpose pack
- Multi-runtime: Claude Cowork (primary), Claude Code CLI, Codex CLI, Gemini CLI, OpenCode, Cursor, Kiro
- Universal skill format allows copy-to-directory installs where marketplace isn't supported
- Slash commands compose multi-skill workflows: `/discover`, `/strategy`, `/write-prd`
- Explicit framework attribution ("rigor of Torres, Cagan, Savoia")

## Why clawfit should care
PM-role fit scoring currently relies on general task match (research, summarization, data-analysis). A PM-specialist skill pack at 13k★ validates that `roles: [pm]` is a meaningful axis with its own high-demand tooling tier. It is the second domain-specific pack after `marketingskills` (23k★) that is not research- or developer-targeted, confirming that L4 skill packs are fragmenting by org role rather than only by domain (finance, academic, security).
