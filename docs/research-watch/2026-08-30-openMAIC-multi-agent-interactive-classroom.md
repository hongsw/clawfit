# Research Watch: OpenMAIC — Open Multi-Agent Interactive Classroom

- Repo/Link: https://github.com/THU-MAIC/OpenMAIC
- Source: GitHub Trending

## Why this is worth watching
OpenMAIC is gaining rapid traction (22k+ stars, +907 today) as a multi-agent orchestration platform oriented around education and interactive learning. It packages the "classroom" metaphor — teacher, student, reviewer agents — as a reusable harness with structured turn-taking and role assignment. This is an early signal that multi-agent patterns are stabilizing around domain-specific role templates.

## What stands out immediately
- TypeScript-first harness with named agent roles and structured dialogue protocols
- Interactive, immersive UX framing that goes beyond chat — agents respond, evaluate, and iterate together
- Modular: any LLM backend can fill each role
- 22k stars with fast growth suggests broad adoption beyond academia

## Why clawfit should care
OpenMAIC is a Layer 2/3 system (harness/wrapper + research loop). It's a high-signal example of multi-agent role templating for a specific domain. The classroom pattern could inform how clawfit categorizes multi-agent task profiles: a coordinator agent, worker agents, and evaluator agents. Also adds weight to `research` and `training/education` as emerging primary task categories.
