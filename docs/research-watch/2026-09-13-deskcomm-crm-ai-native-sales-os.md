# Research Watch: DeskcommCRM — Open-Source AI Sales OS

- Repo/Link: https://github.com/melgarafael/DeskcommCRM
- Source: GitHub Trending

## Why this is worth watching
DeskcommCRM is a self-hosted, open-source CRM that ships with native AI agents and deep WhatsApp integration (via WAHA), positioning itself as an open alternative to Kommo, Octadesk, and Intercom. With 504 stars gained in a single day, it is the fastest-growing AI-native CRM project currently on GitHub Trending. It represents the pattern of vertical SaaS products rebuilding their core around embedded agents rather than bolting AI on top.

## What stands out immediately
- Native AI agents built into the CRM architecture — not a plugin or add-on
- WhatsApp (WAHA) integration for agent-driven customer conversations at scale
- TypeScript implementation, self-hosted, targeting SMB and startup sales orgs
- Explicitly positioned as open-source alternative to commercial AI CRM players
- 504 stars/day velocity is high for a new CRM project

## Why clawfit should care
This signals growing demand for AI agents embedded into domain-specific workflows beyond coding. Clawfit's org-fit taxonomy should track sales/CRM as an emerging `primary_task` category alongside `code-gen` and `research`. The self-hosted nature also informs the `network: offline/hybrid` and `data_sensitivity: confidential` axes — a sales OS that handles customer PII and lives on-premise is a governance-heavy workload.
