# Research Watch: Discovery Loop

- Repo/Link: https://discoveryloop.com
- Source: Hacker News (536 pts, 2026-08-06)

## Why this is worth watching
Jeff Dean, Sanjay Ghemawat, Quoc Le, and Oriol Vinyals — collectively responsible for TensorFlow, Gemini, AutoML-Zero, and foundational distributed systems infrastructure — left Google on August 5, 2026 to co-found Discovery Loop. Their stated goal is to automate the scientific experimental loop itself: proposing, executing, and learning from thousands of experiments simultaneously. The departure generated massive coverage and signals a meaningful bet that the next frontier isn't a better LLM but an automated research engine.

## What stands out immediately
- Founding team is arguably the single highest-credential group to co-found an AI startup
- Focus is on ML research automation first, then hardware design, drug discovery, and clean energy
- Runs thousands of experiments in parallel to compress iteration time dramatically
- Google is both letting them go and backing them as a founding investor + Cloud partner
- Seed round co-led by Radical Ventures and Khosla Ventures (amount undisclosed)
- Positioned as automating the scientific method, not just assisting it

## Why clawfit should care
Discovery Loop is the highest-profile instantiation of L5 (research/evaluation loop automation) in the corpus. It treats the "propose-execute-learn" loop as an engineering substrate — exactly the pattern that clawfit's agent evaluation layer tracks. If their methods propagate as an open API or hosted service, they would become a research-loop harness competing with (or complementing) tools like AutoResearch. The founding team's credibility makes this a near-certain inflection point for the category.
