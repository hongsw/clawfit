## Why this is worth watching
Google Research's Paper Orchestra is a multi-agent framework purpose-built to automate the full AI research paper writing pipeline — literature review, drafting, iteration, and citation. With 1,479 stars and 313 gained today, it signals growing mainstream interest in multi-agent orchestration for structured long-form output, not just chat or code-gen.

## What stands out immediately
- Fully automated research paper workflow from query to draft
- Multi-agent coordination with specialized roles (reviewer, writer, citation agent)
- From Google Research, lending credibility and longevity signal
- Python-native, designed for academic/enterprise research contexts
