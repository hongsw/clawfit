## Why this is worth watching

At 14,349 stars and GitHub Trending rank 2 across all languages on 2026-07-22, this open-source book is the highest-star structured engineering reference for AI agents observed in this scan series. The star count is not primarily a tooling signal — it is a discipline maturation signal: a practitioner community large enough that a 10-chapter engineering text with 88 executable projects achieves mass-trending velocity. This is the second signal for "agent engineering as a formal discipline" after the April 2026 "Harness Engineering" paradigm-naming essay.

## What stands out immediately

- **Organizing formula**: "Agent = LLM + Context + Tools" — collapses the 7-layer taxonomy into three engineering handles; trades precision for teachability
- **Scope spans the taxonomy**: 10 chapters cover context engineering (L5), tools (L4), coding agents (L2), evaluation, model post-training, self-evolution, multimodal interaction (L6), and multi-agent collaboration (L2/L3)
- **88 accompanying projects (70+ independently runnable)**: executable artifacts alongside the text reduce the theory-to-deployment gap; this is an engineering text, not a survey paper
- **Multi-language from launch**: Chinese primary with English, Traditional Chinese, Tamil, and Vietnamese translations — cross-region distribution intent is explicit, not reactive
- **Apache 2.0 on full text**: licenses derivative curricula and course adoption at scale
- **Author**: Li Bojie (Chief Scientist, Pine AI; USTC affiliation) — practitioner-academic positioning gives the text credibility across both engineering and research audiences
