## Why this is worth watching
The framing of open-weight AI following Kubernetes's trajectory — from niche infrastructure to the default deployment substrate — is a credible macro-shift signal. If correct, it implies that proprietary cloud LLM APIs become one option among many, not the default, which directly reshapes the clawfit network/latency dimension.

## What stands out immediately
- 296 points and 239 HN comments suggests broad resonance across the engineering community
- The Kubernetes analogy: complex and initially enterprise-only → eventually commoditized and self-hosted
- Signals growing comfort with running production workloads on open-weight models
- Parallels the shift from proprietary databases to Postgres

## Why clawfit should care
The `network` dimension in clawfit filters (`online` / `hybrid` / `offline`) becomes more important as users shift toward self-hosted open-weight deployments. Tools marked `online`-only that are actually self-hostable may need reclassification. The scoring system should probably weight `hybrid` tools higher for mid-to-large orgs under data-sensitivity pressure.
